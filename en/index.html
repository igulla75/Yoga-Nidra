Breathing and relaxation

Techniques with scientific evidence, explained honestly. Companion to the Yoga Nidra course.

Important. This app is for information only and does not replace medical advice. If you have respiratory or heart conditions, epilepsy, are pregnant, or are being treated for anxiety or depression, choose techniques with your doctor. If you feel unwell, stop and breathe normally.
How to read the evidence levels
● Moderate several reviews or meta-analyses agree, despite limitations.
● Limited few studies or small ones.
● Weak mostly hypotheses or uncertain results.
The level is my own summary of what the sources state, not an official classification. I included only what I read: where I could not read the source, I say so.
